Weekly scorecard · reviewed every Sunday

Scale what works. Kill what doesn’t.

All thresholds below are ESTIMATE proposals. “Rolling 7 days” = the last 7 days at any moment.

Blueprint e-com

  • Target: 4 sales a week.
  • Scale ≥4 sales and ≤$1,500 per sale (rolling 7 days) → release $1,000 of the locked $4,000.
  • Kill ≤1 sale after $5,000 spent (rolling 7 days) → cut that audience, move money to leads.

Leads

  • Target: 40 a day at ≤$12.50.
  • Scale ≤$12.50 per lead for 3 days and the rep reaches ≥60% of leads.
  • Kill >$20 per lead for 3 days, or 0 sales from the first 300 leads.

Masterclass

  • Target: ≤$100 per seat, then Blueprint upgrades.
  • Kill >$150 per seat after $2,000 spent → email and WhatsApp only.
  • Stop all masterclass ads on Nov 14.

Glossary

CPA
Cost per acquisition: ad spend ÷ sales.
CPL
Cost per lead: ad spend ÷ leads.
ROAS
Return on ad spend: revenue ÷ spend.
Retargeting
Showing ads again to people who already visited or engaged.
Lookalike
A Meta audience of people similar to our buyers.
Meta-attributed
Sales Meta credits to its ads via the tracking pixel.
CRM
eTeacher’s customer system (ActiveCampaign), where paid orders are recorded.
OpenCEX
The other company that received leads from 22 Oct.
